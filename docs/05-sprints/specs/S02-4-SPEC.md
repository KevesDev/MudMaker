## S02.4a WorkspaceNavigator
- One `WorkspaceNavigator` owns the destination (`splash`, `create`, `play`, `host`), the transition requests and per-workspace UI session retention (layout, selection and local draft state survive switching; nothing is destroyed on switch). It owns no host, ECS, worker, editor undo stack or client adapter. The `ui.nav.*` actions are owned by it; the window frame actions (minimize, maximize, close) move from the home router to the shared application shell router per the tracker.
- Create, Play and Host open real workspace shells. Each shell is the shared frame row plus one dominant working surface that is **plain and empty** until its feature exists (no fake content, no placeholder text, no empty-state cards: docs 15 and 16). The splash Create, Play and Host controls now activate navigation (the "not available yet" notice goes away for them).
- Switching is explicit and instant when no guard applies; focus moves to the new workspace's primary landmark; back/forward semantics are not required.
## S02.4b Shared toolbar: Go To and Settings
- The frame row's `middle` and `end` slots carry, in Create, Play and Host: at the start the workspace identity as plain text (not a link); at the end `Settings` and `Go To` menus. **Go To lists the other two workspaces plus Home** (current workspace not offered), per docs 10 and 15. **Settings has Appearance** (the existing preset choice, applied through the ThemeManager, scoped as today) in the same place in every workspace. Menus reuse the shared Menu primitive and the S02.2 behaviors (anchored, one column, Escape, focus return, keyboard). Window controls stay at the far end.
- New visible elements get catalog entries with full metadata, registered actions with one owner each, and test ids; counts and drift tests update. No tooltips on ordinary labelled controls (doc 02 principle).
- The splash keeps its own footer Settings and Quit as built.
## S02.4c Transition guard
- Before switching, the navigator asks the owning services for a typed leave impact: `EditorSessionController.describeLeaveImpact()`, `ClientSessionController.describeLeaveImpact()` / `disconnectAll()`, `HostSupervisorSession.describeLeaveImpact()` (interfaces defined in `app/src/nav/`, no implementations in this phase because those services arrive later; production wiring registers none, so no dialog appears yet). When any service reports impact, one combined dialog (shared Dialog) lists the affected resources and offers exactly the choices of the guard table in doc 00 (Create unsaved: Keep Draft and Switch, Save and Switch, Cancel; Play connected: Disconnect and Switch, Cancel; Host running: Keep Hosting and Switch, Stop Hosting and Switch with second confirmation, Cancel). The switch completes only after the chosen service operation confirms; failure blocks the switch and shows the error with retry; Cancel leaves everything untouched; versions are rechecked before commit. Tested with fake services (all guard-table rows, races, failure, cancel); the real services are later phases.
- App exit is separate (the Quit and close path keeps its own future stop-hosting guard): not in this phase.
